# Flood & Disaster Response

## Flooding Reminder

During heavy rain or typhoons:

- Avoid walking through flooded streets.
- Report clogged drainage immediately.
- Monitor barangay announcements through AlalAI or official Facebook pages.
- Prepare emergency kits and backup power banks.

# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## How long does barangay clearance processing take?

Typical processing time is 10–20 minutes depending on queue volume.

---

## Can I file complaints anonymously?

Yes, but anonymous complaints may require additional verification.

---

## What should I do during flooding?

Move to higher ground and report dangerous areas immediately through AlalAI or emergency hotlines.

---

## How do I request a certificate of residency?

Bring a valid ID and proof of residence to the barangay hall.

---

## Is the Libreng Tuli program free?

Yes. The program is fully sponsored by the barangay health office.
